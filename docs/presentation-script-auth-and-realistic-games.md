# BIG CRUISE〽️ — Presentation Script
## Authentication Retention, Realistic Game Visuals and 7 Days of Cruise

### Opening

“Welcome to the latest BIG CRUISE〽️ update: a smoother return experience, a more cinematic arcade, and a mobile-first visual system that makes every day of the Cruise feel like its own room.”

### 1. Login once, stay in the Cruise

“Members now get a retained login session. Supabase persists the session in the browser, refreshes tokens automatically, and restores the member on a later visit. That means returning Cruisers do not need to re-enter their details every time.”

“Security stays in the member’s hands: the app now exposes a clear Sign out action, and signing out ends the session normally.”

### 2. Read-only preview for demos and QA

“For design reviews and mobile testing, we added an explicit read-only preview route. Opening `/?preview=1` lets anyone inspect the seven-day experience without creating fake authentication, changing member data, or saving game results.”

“This gives product, design and community teams a fast way to review the app while keeping the real account boundary intact.”

### 3. The arcade now looks like a real game platform

“The emoji-only game tiles have been upgraded with a cinematic visual pack: a premium card table, a detailed strategy board, a neon party room, a karaoke stage microphone and a quiz-night mobile scene.”

“The art is mapped by game family so card games, strategy games, social games, karaoke and quizzes each communicate their own energy before the player taps.”

### 4. Seven Days of Cruise becomes a visual system

“The seven-day carousel now transitions across seven brand-locked rooms, each with its own artwork, motif, accent color, mood and featured games.”

“DOMINION STATE brings quiet power, deep wine tones and strategy energy. CHAOS CULTURE flips the room into lime, orange and high-contrast wildcard energy. Both transitions were tested on a 390 by 844 mobile viewport using the live production build.”

### 5. Solo bots and user multiplayer

“The existing Ludo, Chess, UNO, Draw It Out and party-game flows already expose the two primary choices: solo Cruise Bot play or a multiplayer room. Kahoot now uses the same bot-plus-room architecture, so it can be played alone or with users.”

“The next multiplayer pass is focused on extending the same authenticated room model to the strategy-board family, while preserving each game’s own rules and controls.”

### 6. BIG CRUISE CONTROL〽️ music layer

“The new Music tab brings the BIG CRUISE CONTROL〽️ playlist into the app through Spotify’s official embed and direct open-in-Spotify link.”

“It also introduces artiste profiles with bios, playlist tracks and a Book Me interaction for preparing a booking brief. Because public Spotify pages do not expose private stream totals, artiste ranking is presented transparently as a playlist-presence discovery proxy—not as claimed stream analytics.”

### Close

“BIG CRUISE〽️ is becoming a complete culture-and-play platform: a persistent member identity, a mobile-first arcade, seven distinct weekly rooms, a richer music layer and a clearer path from discovery to participation.”
